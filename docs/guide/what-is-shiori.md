# What is Shiori?

Shiori is a self-hosted workspace for teams. Keep boards, documents, spreadsheets, and presentations in the same Project, with shared access and version history.

## How teams use Shiori

- **Organize work in Workshop.** Create personal and Team Projects. Use Inbox, Calendar, and Recents to keep track of activity. Search for a file or its contents and open the matching object, block, cell, or slide.
- **Work together.** Sketch on a board, write a brief in Docs, track numbers in Sheets, or present from Slides. Leave comments, share with roles, and name versions for review.
- **Move your work.** Import and export supported Markdown, Word, CSV, Excel, and PowerPoint files. Move a Project with its file history and referenced private images in a [Project archive](./project-archive.md).
- **Run it on your infrastructure.** The Compose release includes the web app, background worker, PostgreSQL, Redis, and SeaweedFS. Follow the [operations guide](./operations.md) to back up, upgrade, or restore an instance.

The [Meetings](./meetings.md) media service and [Board Assistant](./board-assistant.md) models are optional. Configure and verify them for your deployment.
